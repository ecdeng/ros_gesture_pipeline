What the robot can physically do

Six channels, straight from the build: yaw ±180° (whole body turns, at most ~170°/s), pitch −25° up to +15° down, roll ±18°, bob ±6 mm, two arm flaps 0–90°. Combined tilt ≤ 28°; big tilts give up bob. Cheeks glow, catchlights blink. No eyelids, no mouth movement. Everything the studio generates is clamped to these.
